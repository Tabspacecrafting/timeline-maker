# Timeline Maker

Make timelines in your browser: events (moments and periods) with tags, colours, notes, photos/videos and links,
shown on a scrollable, zoomable axis.

**[Open Timeline Maker](https://tabspacecrafting.github.io/timeline-maker/)**

- Timelines by date and time, years (up to billions of years), calendar year, plain numbers, or your own units.
- Vertical or horizontal, with zoom, a mini timeline and five example timelines to try.
- Everything is saved in your browser; use "Save to file" to keep or share a timeline.

It is a static website with no backend and nothing to install. To run it locally, open `index.html` from this folder.
