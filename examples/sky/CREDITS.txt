Pictures for the "A year in the sky" example.

The four season diagrams (march-equinox.svg, summer-solstice.svg, september-equinox.svg,
winter-solstice.svg) were drawn for this site: the Sun, Earth's orbit, and where the Earth is on it,
with its tilted axis and its night side. They are part of the site and need no credit.
